# CSM Ostrava screenshot handoff

The product owner selected the final phone and tablet screenshots in the local
preview and requested their App Store preparation on 2026-08-04. The canonical
Fastlane directory is intentionally flat: Fastlane detects Apple display types
from PNG dimensions, while the `iphone69-` and `ipad13-` filename prefixes keep
the repository preflight deterministic.

The images show the approved CSM visual identity, public event/program content,
public speaker imagery, and the non-privileged demonstration profile Jan Novák.
They contain no passwords, authentication tokens, or administrative screens.
